New and changed

- Pocket Assistant now uses three tabs in Devices / Capture / Head Track order. Capture combines a compact record button with gimbal controls, and the UI follows the iPhone's light or dark appearance. Head Track includes supported-headphones help, gimbal recenter, and Fast / Standard / Gentle presets.
- Gamepad: left stick drives the gimbal; Cross/A records, Circle/B recenters, Square/X rotates 180°, Triangle/Y tracks/cancels, L1/R1 jumps zoom, L2/R2 holds zoom, and D-pad changes ISO/shutter. Unplug rests the stick; reaching a gimbal stop taps once.
- Experimental head tracking is in Operator Setup → Controls. Wear motion-capable AirPods, go live, hold still and calibrate. Head turn pans 1:1; nod tilts; roll is read-only. STOP clears the lock.
- The on-screen gimbal stick uses the same gradual speed.
- Pair over Bluetooth, join the camera's Wi-Fi, and watch a live view. Record, ISO, white balance, and zoom from the phone.
- Share Diagnostics is on first pair and in Operator Setup → System; its report contains no name or location.

Fixes

- Pocket Assistant restarts a headphone-motion stream that reports active but produces no first sample, then falls back to the Core Motion pull path. It gives an in-ear/retry instruction if both paths remain empty.
- In D-Log2, zoom waits for the D-Log hop; the chip stays at 1× until color switches. Zoom keeps the picture. COLOR lists only camera-supported modes.
- Pan, tilt, head tracking, record, FORMAT, COLOR, white balance, and tracking boxes must not freeze live view. Recovery holds the last frame and reconnects without closing the app.
- Head tracking no longer bobs past where you look and swings back. Turning your head, then nodding, tilts straight up and down. Rapid look-pause-look gestures no longer freeze the picture.
- Power-cycling, zooming with assists, and opening clips or Setup no longer replace live view with black.
- On Pocket 3 and Pocket 4, Auto ISO ranges now start at 50, matching the camera. Pocket 4 Pro still starts at 100.
- Zebra Highlight now catches any hot color channel. Factory Highlight is 99 IRE; saved thresholds stay unchanged. Its number fields remain above the keyboard.

What to test

- In Pocket Assistant, verify the Devices / Capture / Head Track tab order. In Capture, test the compact record control, joystick, recenter, rotate 180°, pose, and speed. Switch iPhone Appearance between Light and Dark while the app is open; every page should update immediately and remain readable.
- Open Head Track → View supported headphones and verify the Chinese AirPods/Beats list. Before calibration, tap Gimbal recenter. Select Fast, Standard, and Gentle and confirm all four sliders update; changing a slider should leave every preset unselected. Re-wear connected AirPods and play audio: AirPods should reach Motion data ready without relaunching the app.
- Pair, go live, and stay two minutes with LUT/waveform. Pan, zoom, record, change FORMAT/COLOR, and track a subject. Set Zebra Highlight to 95 in portrait and landscape. Frozen picture with live timecode is a bug; Reconnecting must hold the last picture.
- On Pocket 4 Pro in D-Log2 (idle): press R2 or pinch. COLOR switches to D-Log first, then the picture zooms. On a Pocket 3, open ISO and switch to Auto: ranges read 50–400 and up, same as the camera. Pocket 4 Pro still starts at 100.
- Tap Share Diagnostics before pairing. Record a take, open it with LUT, and share the original. Return from Library and Setup, then power-cycle: live view must return while the last picture is held.
